# Welcome to Folio

**Folio** is your private, local-first personal reading library. Everything stays on your device—no accounts, no trackers, no ads, and zero network permissions.

## Supported Formats

- **Reflowable eBooks:** `EPUB`, `MOBI`, `AZW3`, `FB2`
- **Documents & Text:** `PDF`, `DOCX`, `RTF`, `TXT`, `HTML`, `Markdown`
- **Comics & Manga:** `CBZ`, `CBR`

## Reading Controls & Gestures

> Tap the center of any page to toggle the reading toolbar.

- **Tap Zones:** Tap the right edge to advance, left edge to go back, or switch to One-Handed / Scroll-Only mode in settings.
- **Volume Keys:** Use hardware volume buttons to turn chapters or pages effortlessly.
- **Edge Brightness:** Slide up or down along the left margin to adjust reading brightness on the fly.
- **Highlights & Notes:** Long-press any paragraph while reading to highlight it in Amber, Sage, Rose, or Sky and attach a personal margin note.

## Organizing Your Library

1. Use **Import Books** to pick one or more files from your device or synced storage (Syncthing, Nextcloud, Drive Files).
2. Use **Link Folder** to scan an entire folder automatically.
3. Group books into custom **Shelves**, add `#tags`, edit metadata & covers, and export your entire library + notes anytime as a `.zip` or Markdown file.

```
Tip: Open the Aa button in the reader to customize fonts, Paper/Sepia/Slate/OLED themes, margins, or start offline Text-to-Speech.
```
